VN Player

Plays game bundles made by the converter on your PC. Everything stays on this device.

Your games

Pick the .vnbundle.zip file. It is copied into this app's storage, so the original can be deleted afterwards.

Full screen

Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, and open the player from the new icon. Import your games there: the Home Screen app keeps its own storage, separate from Safari, and iPadOS does not clear it after a week of not being used.
